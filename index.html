About

I'm a full-stack developer with a designer's eye and an unreasonable love for the details most people never notice.

For 7+ years I've partnered with startups and studios to ship interfaces that feel alive — from design systems used by thousands to one-page sites that punch way above their weight. My stack of choice is lean: semantic HTML, modern CSS, and vanilla JavaScript whenever it gets the job done.

When I'm not online, you'll find me shooting street photography, over-engineering my coffee setup, or losing at chess.
